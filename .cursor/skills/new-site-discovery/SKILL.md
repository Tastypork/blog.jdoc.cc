# New site discovery

Collect or infer only what is explicitly supported:
- Business name and verified contact details
- Audience and service area
- Primary services and differentiators
- Main conversion goal and secondary actions
- Required pages and must-have features
- Brand assets, preferred style, and visual references
- Existing domain, hosting, analytics, forms, and integrations
- Client-provided proof such as testimonials, case studies, certifications, or photos
- Timeline, content owner, and approval process

Then produce in docs/ in base of repo:
1. client-brief.md: A concise project brief.
2. sitemap.md: Sitemap and page goals.
3. visuals.md: Proposed visual direction and design system.
4. assumptions.md: Scope boundaries and assumptions.

Do not invent reviews, results, certifications, pricing, or guarantees. Keep discovery focused and ask only the questions that materially affect design or implementation.
